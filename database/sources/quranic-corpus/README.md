- The file is kept byte-for-byte (SHA-256 in SHA256SUMS). The site reads it at build time and shows per-word part of speech, root and features. Arabic labels for tags follow the corpus tagset documentation.
- Word alignment: words are matched to our Tanzil Uthmani text by position. 4 ayat differ in word division (2:181, 8:6, 13:37, 37:130); for these no analysis is shown.
- Status: automatic annotation by the corpus (partly reviewed by its volunteers); shown as a learning aid, not as i'rab or tafsir.
